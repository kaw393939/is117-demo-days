# Demo Days: show what you built

Start with a problem your project solves. Show someone doing one useful thing, then explain a choice you made and the evidence behind it. A working example beats a long tour of the menus.

## Your presentation time

**Canvas is the only source for dates, slots, length, and delivery details.** Choose your section: [005](/demo-days/section-005) · [007](/demo-days/section-007).

## Required PowerPoint support

Keep the slides short enough to leave time for the live work and questions. Prepare one short individual deck for both solo projects (T-shirt store and portfolio), and one shared deck per team for the group project. Check your final-product assignments in Canvas for the requirements.

- **Problem:** Who needed your site, and what were they trying to do?
- **Solution:** Let us see the task from start to finish.
- **Evidence:** What changed after you watched someone try it? What happened when they tried again?
- **Next step:** What would you improve, and why? In a group, say what each person built and reviewed.

For example: “Our classmate picked a size but couldn't find the measurements. We moved the guide next to the size buttons. On the retest, they picked a size without asking.” [See a worked example](/examples/submissions/campus-cotton#_4-get-feedback-and-improve).

## Rehearse one task

1. **Show the task.** Pick one useful action the audience can watch from start to finish.
2. **Open the evidence.** Show the issue, change, or test that affected a decision. For a team demo, each person opens something they worked on and explains their role.
3. **Explain the result.** Say what a reviewer did, what you changed, and what you saw on the retry. Be ready to explain where AI helped and what you checked yourself.

## Before you present

- Open the site, repository, and evidence links from the same record you submitted.
- Try the main workflow yourself. For the T-shirt shop, know how to show the actual local SQLite behavior if the public preview only simulates it.
- Rehearse one change and its retest. Explain your own choices and where AI helped.
- Check Canvas for the due date, assessment details, and any instructions for a remaining presentation or redo. The no-submission final rows reuse your existing work; no extra report or upload is needed. [See the rubric](/course/milestones#how-each-step-is-graded).
