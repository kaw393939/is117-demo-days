### Compare three sites

Source pages checked November 2, 2026. These are observations, not a purchase test or proof of demand. These brands have larger catalogs; we compare selected white T-shirt pages.

| Reference | Observation → lesson |
| --- | --- |
| [Fresh Clean Tees](https://freshcleantees.com/products/white-crew-neck) | Length choices and fit information. Put measurements next to size selection. |
| [True Classic](https://www.trueclassictees.com/products/white-crew-neck-tee) | Product options and membership pricing paths. Explain the single-item offer without extra pricing paths. |
| [Buck Mason](https://www.buckmason.com/products/white-slub-curved-hem-tee) | Specific fabric and production details. Use specific product facts rather than vague premium claims. |

**Two possible positions:** A — Affordable everyday essentials. B — Campus club bulk supplier.

### Compare two personas

| | Persona 1 | Persona 2 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Situation | A student buying an everyday white tee online | A club organizer comparing shirts for an event |
| Need | Choose a size and understand the total without hunting for information. | Bulk ordering and custom printing |
| Main concern | Will it fit, and what will I actually pay? | Will this site focus on my task? |

**Evidence versus assumption:** Reference sites show how others present useful information. We hypothesize that these visitors need it too; we have not interviewed them.

**Choice:** Choose the individual shopper. Bulk and custom orders would expand the project beyond one clear purchase journey.

## 2. Plan

<details>
<summary>See two short website plans and the user stories</summary>

| Plan A | Plan B |
| --- | --- |
| Shop first: price, measurements, and size selection appear together. | Story first: a campus-day narrative leads into the same product and checkout. |

**Brand:** Everyperson; plain, practical, welcoming. Both plans support the same core task and use the stories below. We change the information order and presentation, rather than doubling the whole project.

| Story | User need | Feature and check for success |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | As a shopper unsure about fit, I want garment measurements beside sizes so I can choose with more confidence. | An expandable measurement guide beside a required size selector. Open the guide, return to the selector, and keep the selected size. |
| 2 | As a price-conscious shopper, I want an itemized total so I can understand the demo purchase. | Quantity control and a summary using one $25 classroom concept price. Two shirts show $50; demo shipping and tax are explicitly $0. |
| 3 | As a shopper, I want a clear confirmation so I know the simulated checkout completed. | Validated checkout followed by a demo reference and stored order. Missing size/email prevents checkout; a valid submission produces a reference. |

**Build with:** HTML, CSS, JavaScript and demo records; browser storage on the public site, SQLite in the local server version.

**Leave for later:** Discount codes, accounts, bulk orders, and real payments.
