## Practice Demo Day interaction

This is a **10-minute pair rehearsal outline**, not a measured live presentation. Maya's three-project set is supplied; Avery must also bring their own individual store and portfolio.

| Time | Evidence |
| --- | --- |
| 0–2 minutes | Maya's portfolio/store, one research-to-feature choice, one feedback change. |
| 2–4 minutes | Avery's own individual work and evidence. |
| 4–8 minutes | Shared business: problem, A/B decision, workflow, work split, cross-review, improvement, and limits. |
| 8–10 minutes | Questions and transition. |

**Instructor:** “What connects the research to the product?”

**Maya:** “Fit uncertainty became a size-guide story and a check. The career reflection helped me choose an internship reviewer as my portfolio reader. The group's research led us to an inquiry flow instead of promising confirmed bookings.”

**Instructor:** “What did you personally do?”

**Maya:** “I implemented service explanation and responsive layouts, then reviewed Avery's form. Avery implemented intake and receipts and reviewed my scope language. We integrated and checked the whole request journey.”

**Instructor:** “Did A/B prove A sells better?”

**Maya:** “No. We made a reasoned classroom choice. Design and information order changed; we have no sales experiment.”

**Instructor:** “Where did AI help?”

**Maya:** “Drafting layout and validation code. I checked the specification against behavior and can explain the form, server checks, and SQLite record. Public static mode uses browser storage.”

## Instructor findings

- **Career research should do one job twice:** satisfy portfolio Research and guide its audience/evidence. Keep the personal reflection private.
- **Stagger the workload:** the group follows about a week behind and uses December 1/3 to catch up. Introduce final review expectations November 24 so catch-up week stays free of new topics.
- **Reuse evidence:** three evolving records, shared A/B code, short Canvas notes, and continuing issues rather than duplicate reports.
- **Check actual workload:** this AI role-play does not establish novice completion time. Ask students about time/blockers early and adjust support or scope from that evidence.
- **Prepare beyond class:** students should practice a short introduction and demo, then use accurate portfolios/profiles in networking and applications. [Start-here guide](/course/overview).
