## 1. Research — due November 17
**Problem hypothesis:** a commuter with a bike problem needs to know how to start without assuming an inquiry is an appointment. No real customer demand has been validated.

| Source checked Nov 2 | Observation | Feature decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Grove Street Bicycles, Jersey City](https://www.grovestreetbicycles.com/about/repair-services-pg67.html) | Lists repair services and separates parts from labor pricing. | Explain inspection/quote and parts rather than promise an all-inclusive price. |
| [REI bike shops](https://www.rei.com/stores/bike-shop) | Explains repair/tune-up services and finding a shop. National adjacent reference. | Explain service scope and an obvious first step. |
| [Trek Philadelphia Manayunk](https://www.trekbikes.com/us/en_US/retail/philadelphia_manayunk/) | Location page has service levels and a drop-off advising path. Not a Newark competitor. | Distinguish a request from confirmed work. Do not copy its turnaround promises. |

**Two positions/personas:** A: commuter Ren needs a clear first contact and preferred drop-off window. B: enthusiast Jo wants detailed upgrades, component inventory and technical comparison. Both invented hypotheses. Choose A; three service categories and one inquiry fit our scope. These observations establish a workflow reference, not a proven local niche.

Modernist layout makes next steps predictable. Caregiver archetype supports calm, helpful language; avoid implying mechanical expertise we lack. Honest persuasion: reciprocity through a clear “what happens next”; commitment/consistency by repeating the chosen service/window in a pending receipt. No invented credentials, testimonials or scarcity. Alternative postmodern campaign styling would be lively but would need to preserve these functional cues.

[Simulated customer brief](/examples/trial-customer) clarified: no calendar guarantee, no same-day promise, no payment. Answers are roleplay, not discovery interviews.
