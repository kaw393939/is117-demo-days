# Competitor site reference gallery

Captured in Chrome on November 2, 2026. These are desktop product-page views at the browser's existing viewport, not a normalized design benchmark or mobile audit. Third-party images are retained for analysis; create or license assets for your own sites.

## Fresh Clean Tees

[White Crew Neck product page](https://freshcleantees.com/products/white-crew-neck).

![Fresh Clean Tees product photograph beside price, reviews, pack, size, and length controls](/reference/fresh-clean-tees-desktop.jpg)

**Observed:** Large gallery and an options panel; model thumbnails include differing body sizes; length selection has short/regular/tall choices. **Interpretation:** Fit confidence is a central part of the offer. **Apply:** Give measurements and model context; a white-only concept does not need numerous color controls.

## True Classic

[Classic Crew Neck product page](https://www.trueclassictees.com/products/white-crew-neck-tee).

![True Classic style comparison row above the shirt gallery and size, length, hem, and pack choices](/reference/true-classic-desktop.jpg)

**Observed:** A row of shirt types precedes the product; size, length, hem, and pack controls occupy the buying panel. Pricing is farther down this viewport. **Interpretation:** Category guidance helps a larger catalog but adds vertical space. **Apply:** A one-product site can prioritize its core product and price.

## Buck Mason

[White Slub Curved Hem Tee product page](https://www.buckmason.com/products/white-slub-curved-hem-tee).

![Buck Mason shirt image beside restrained product details, manufacturing context, and size selection](/reference/buck-mason-desktop.jpg)

**Observed:** Manufacturing context appears near the title and price; details use expandable sections. Some sizes were unavailable at capture. **Interpretation:** Restraint and concrete production details support craftsmanship positioning. **Apply:** Explain product facts in plain language; make unavailable states understandable beyond color alone.

## Cuts

[AO Curve-Hem Tee product page](https://www.cutsclothing.com/products/white-crew-curve-hem).

![Cuts flat and worn product photographs beside color, size, style, and one-time versus subscription pricing](/reference/cuts-desktop.jpg)

**Observed:** Product-only and worn photos sit side by side; one-time and subscription purchases are distinct. A full-screen email promotion was dismissed before capture. **Interpretation:** The images explain fit, while multiple offers add complexity. **Apply:** Show both product and context; let people inspect the product without submitting contact details.

## Limits of this reference

No products were purchased, worn, or washed. No checkout, full policy, mobile, performance, or formal accessibility audit was performed. Observing a review count does not establish sales volume or independent product quality. Read each original page for current details.
