# Suggested business projects

These are classroom adaptations of work described in [local internship postings](/career/internships), not employer assignments or endorsements. Choose one useful workflow and keep it achievable. Use synthetic data, especially for examples inspired by healthcare or HR.

| Project | Small but meaningful scope | Collaboration opportunity | Career connection |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Tutoring / service appointment requests | Explain services; collect preferences; store a request; let staff review status | One member owns the visitor flow; another owns the request-management flow; exchange QA | Coinbase's internal apps and workflow improvement |
| Local contractor quote assistant | Collect project details; calculate a transparent estimate; save a lead | Split quote logic and customer interface from lead review/data flow | Healthpeak's business-team software and rapid prototypes |
| Club or small-business onboarding | Registration checklist, missing-information feedback, status summary | Split applicant journey and staff review, then test the handoff | Dime's onboarding and integration work |
| Customer inquiry triage | Inquiry form, categories, staff queue, response draft | Split intake/validation and queue/status; add optional AI drafting only after the basic flow works | Interplay's scoped tools and operational workflows |
| Fictional document intake service | Upload/select synthetic sample documents; extract or manually capture fields; flag missing details | Split intake UI and review queue; jointly define correctness checks | Beacon/Ascend's document and exception-handling workflows |
| Event registration business | Event details, capacity-aware signup, confirmation, organizer list | Split public registration and organizer flow; exchange edge-case testing | Customer-facing web apps, data, and adoption |
| Small operations dashboard | Import a sample CSV; show useful measures; filter and inspect exceptions | Split data validation and dashboard; review each other's assumptions | Datasite's business discovery and AI transformation |

## Keep the business story credible

Explain who uses it, what they do now, how your tool helps, and how you would measure improvement. If no real user was interviewed, label the customer and workflow assumptions. An instructor or peer can act as the client; record their feedback honestly.

## Features that can wait

Complex authentication, real payments, autonomous external actions, large integrations, custom model training, and elaborate dashboards can consume the semester without improving the core demo. Defer anything that prevents you from completing and verifying the main journey.

## Example success criteria

“A visitor submits a complete request, sees a confirmation, and staff can find that request.” Measure successful completion and error handling during a peer review. A claim such as “saves 50% of staff time” needs an actual baseline and measurement; do not invent business results.
