## What to work on in each cycle

- **Research:** revisit three sites through design, brand character, and trust; compare two audience/need hypotheses. Bring them to the first feedback class.
- **Plan:** choose the promise, voice, visual approaches, and two honest persuasion elements; connect them to stories and checks in the same document. Reuse Brand Studio.
- **Build:** make those decisions visible in the two working versions. Shared content and code are fine.
- **Improve:** have a peer explain the offer and attempt the task; make a meaningful change and retest.

These are focuses within the existing milestones, not extra submissions. [See the short strategy example](/process/find-your-angle).

**November 30–December 3: no new topic.** Use class to finish projects, catch up on individual and group reviews, get peer feedback, fix problems, and rehearse. Bring the work that still needs attention.

No new submission is due during Thanksgiving recess.

## What happens during a feedback class?

Use the [five-step collaboration and review routine](/process/collaboration). Rotate roles, choose an important observation, record a response, and retry the change. The timings below are a suggested class format, not a quota for every project.

| Time | Activity |
| --- | --- |
| 10 minutes | Instructions and questions |
| 20 minutes | One partner shows work; the other attempts a task and gives feedback |
| 20 minutes | Switch roles, or exchange group projects with another team |
| 25 minutes | Make a useful change and check it |
| 5 minutes | Record what happened and the next step |

Spread the three final walkthroughs across the available meetings. Each project needs its own feedback-driven change. In a trio, share time so everyone participates. Contact the instructor privately in Canvas about absences or accommodations, following course/university policies.
