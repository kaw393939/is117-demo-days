## What to work on in each cycle

Work on the T-shirt shop and portfolio as individual projects first. Then bring the same four steps to a lean team sprint: Research, Plan, Build, Improve. [What to hand in](/course/milestones#what-to-submit-at-each-step).

## Career reflection starts the portfolio

[Compare two roles](/career/explore), choose a reader for your portfolio, and use your own evidence. Keep the full reflection private in Canvas.

## Our weekly rhythm

Learn the task, try it with a classmate, and improve the same project. Canvas says when to submit.

## Week before demos: work and catch up

Use the time your instructor identifies in Canvas to fix issues, check links, and rehearse rather than adding new features. [Demo checklist](/demo-days/#before-you-present).

## Calendar exceptions {#thanksgiving-changes-the-last-cycle}

Follow the current Canvas schedule for your section when the normal class pattern changes.

## What happens during a feedback class?

Give your peer one specific task. Watch before you help. Record the observation, make a change, and retest it. [See how to run a review](/process/collaboration).
