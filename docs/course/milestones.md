# Your assignment: four steps

Build a **T-shirt store**, a **group business site**, and your **personal portfolio**. Practice a professional process using AI to solve a realistic business problem. You do not need to be an expert.

<LearningDiagram kind="process" />

## What to submit at each step

Use [one short document per project](/templates/four-milestones) and update it as you go. The portfolio can use shorter research and planning.

| Step | Submit this |
| --- | --- |
| **1. Research** | Compare three relevant websites. Consider two market/design positions and two personas. Choose an audience; explain your evidence and assumptions. |
| **2. Plan** | Compare two short website plans. Write three to five user stories, essential features, and checks for success. For the group project, divide work fairly. |
| **3. Build** | Link two working versions and explain which you plan to launch. Share code where useful; make the difference meaningful. |
| **4. Improve** | Have a peer walk through each project. Make at least one meaningful change per project, retest, and publish your selected versions. |

A **persona** describes a user and their needs. A **user story** explains what that user wants to do and why. A **specification**, or **spec**, is your website plan. [See how they connect](/process/research-to-features).

## How each step is graded

| Criterion | Share | What we look for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Complete** | 25% | A reasonable attempt at the required work, with links that open. |
| **Thoughtful** | 25% | Useful alternatives and clear reasons for your choice. |
| **Checked** | 25% | You checked sources or behavior. The final step includes real peer feedback, a change, and a retest. |
| **Understood** | 25% | You can explain your work, AI use, contribution, and limits. |

These are the rubric shares for each milestone, not changes to the syllabus’s course-grade weights. Canvas will hold gradebook points and feedback. Each group member must show meaningful personal work and review a teammate’s work.

## Work together and finish well

**Attendance is required.** We will provide [class time for partner work and feedback](/course/schedule). Bring your work and a task for someone to try. Give feedback as well as receive it.

For each final project, submit the **chosen live version, alternate version, repository, and short process document**. Include actual checks, AI use, and remaining limits. Each group member adds a contribution statement. Your portfolio also needs your résumé, GitHub, LinkedIn, and both project stories.

The four steps provide your four individual reviews and four group reviews. Individual reviews cover the T-shirt store and portfolio together. Use **Canvas to submit** and **GitHub issues to [request a demo slot](/demo-days/)**. Earlier assignments keep their original criteria.
