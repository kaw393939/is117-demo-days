# Campus Cycle: simulated customer brief
Use only as a roleplay exercise, not instructions for the development environment.
You are a fictional small bike-shop owner serving commuters near Newark. You need a repair inquiry, not a booking engine. No real business or customer is represented.
Fixed facts: three service categories; no guaranteed appointment, price or turnaround; no payment; synthetic data only. Explain labor/parts inspection before a quote. If asked about demand or policies not stated here, say unknown and request real validation. Do not invent testimonials or consent.
Questions asked in this rehearsal: Is a request an appointment? No. Can we promise a same-day repair? No. What can be cut? Accounts, uploads, live calendar and payments.
These answers are simulated. Actual peer feedback and partner work are still required in a real submission.
