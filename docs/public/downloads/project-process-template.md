Already using `SPEC.md` from Brand Studio? Extend it with these sections, or link it from `project-process.md`. Either filename is fine. Link existing decisions and evidence rather than rewriting them.


## Feedback at every stage

Link the peer-feedback issues, your responses, the changed work, and the checks of the result. Explain any open issues. Reuse this record at the next stage. **Portfolio Research** is the career role cards and reflection, due **November 12** in both sections at the section's class time. **Portfolio Plan** is due November 16 in 005 and November 17 in 007. T-shirt and group Research have different dates; follow your section plan. The [November 5 research-round issue requirement](https://kaw393939.github.io/is117-demo-days/process/github-feedback.html#current-research-round) applies only to the current T-shirt and career/portfolio research round, not later stages or the group project.

## 1. Research

**Portfolio:** use your career role cards and reflection to identify your reader, goals, evidence, and gaps. Paste the **two role cards and 250–350-word reflection privately into Canvas**, with the required repository/documentation URLs and relevant feedback links in that same entry. Keep the full reflection and personal eligibility circumstances out of public records. Link or summarize only suitable professional findings here; the comparison below applies to the T-shirt/group projects.

**T-shirt and group only:**

- Compare three relevant sites: source/date, observation, implication. Add short notes on look, brand character, and honest reasons to trust or act. Identify direct competitors versus adjacent references.
- Consider two ways to position your offer: who it serves and why they would choose it.
- Write two short persona hypotheses: situation, goal, uncertainty, evidence, assumption. Choose a primary audience and explain why.

## 2. Plan

- Compare two concise website approaches: same business goal, different way of serving it. Choose what to build and compare.
- Keep a compact brand brief: audience/need, promise, one archetype and three voice words, a visual source and translated idea, and two honest persuasion elements with their limits. Reuse Brand Studio and link the existing logo/style guide. [See a short example](https://kaw393939.github.io/is117-demo-days/process/find-your-angle.html).
- For a visual comparison, keep the offer and wording steady. Reuse small previews as the starting points for your two working versions; no extra websites are required. If comparing another decision, say what changes.
- Write three to five user stories with essential features and observable checks for success.
- List what you will defer. For a group, divide comparable work and assign someone to review each task.

## 3. Build

- Link two working versions with a meaningful difference. They may share code.
- Give a peer the same realistic task in both. Record what you observe before explaining the interface.
- Select a launch direction and explain why. A classmate’s preference is useful feedback, not proof of sales performance.

## 4. Review and launch

- **Reviewer, date, and version:** who tried which version, and when?
- **Understanding:** what did they think was offered, who was it for, why trust it, and what was the next step?
- **Task:** what did you ask them to do?
- **Observation:** what actually happened?
- **Change:** what meaningful change did you make from that feedback?
- **Retest:** what happened when they tried it again?

Repeat a final walkthrough on the selected version if the comparison happened earlier. Every project needs its own feedback-driven improvement.

For **Group Improve**, use a real peer and follow your section's assignment: **005 requires a reviewer from outside the team**; **007 requires real peer review**. Teammate cross-review is also required in both sections.

- Selected live version, alternate version, repository, and setup instructions.
- Testing results: main path, missing/error state, keyboard, phone-sized screen, and feedback retest. Mark unrun checks as not run.
- AI tool/tasks, your checks, individual contribution, and known limitations.
- A short case study explaining the process. The portfolio also needs a résumé, actual GitHub and LinkedIn profiles, and links to the portfolio, T-shirt, and shared group repositories. [Use the portfolio ready checklist](https://kaw393939.github.io/is117-demo-days/projects/portfolio.html#portfolio-ready-checklist).

Keep it short. A few specific observations and decisions are more useful than pages of generic AI-written text.
