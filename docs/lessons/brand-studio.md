# Brand Studio: same shirt, two designs

**Why it matters:** A design choice is easier to test when you change one thing at a time. Keep the same shirt and offer. See what changes when you change the page.

## Try it

1. Pick one shopper and one question. For example, a commuter wants to know whether the shirt will fit before buying it.
2. Write one short brand promise. The shopper tells you what information matters; the brand character tells you how to say it. Don’t invent a certification, fabric quality, or customer quote.
3. Use the same shirt, copy, and action in two layouts. Put fit information by the size picker in one version and elsewhere in the other. Let someone try choosing a size without coaching.
4. Write down what happened. Choose a layout for a reason you can show and check it again.

## Check your work

Could the shopper explain the offer and choose a size? Explain one design choice in your own words. Save what actually happened, even if the version you preferred didn’t work best.
